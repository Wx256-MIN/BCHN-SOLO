BCHN-SOLO

CKPool-derived Bitcoin Cash solo mining pool. This is a setup page, not a monitoring dashboard.

1. Set your payout address

Enter a mainnet BCH CashAddr you control. Double-check it carefully. The address is saved on this Umbrel in the pool's persistent app data.

2. Configure your miner

Pool URL: stratum+tcp://YOUR_UMBREL_LAN_IP:3333

Username: your valid BCH CashAddr, optionally followed by .workername

Password: x

This pool defaults to a 0% operator fee. Shares are not blocks; a block candidate must be accepted by BCHN. Keep RPC private and only expose Stratum to networks you trust.
